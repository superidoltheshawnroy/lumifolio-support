What to expect from photo recognition and location checks.

Updated November 11, 2026

01Exploration and collection

LumiFolio helps you discover city landmarks, light up their cards, and build your collection. Photo recognition and location checks are part of the exploration experience.

02Recognition and its limits

Recognition may identify the wrong subject or miss a match, so not every photo will light up a landmark. Lighting, angle, obstructions, photo quality, and reference artwork can affect the result. Recognition supports the in-app exploration and collection experience; it does not guarantee the identity of the subject in a photo. Try another angle, different lighting, or another photo.

03Location results

Lighting up a landmark using your location depends on system permission, location accuracy, and your surroundings. If your location is uncertain or you are not near the landmark, the check may fail. Try again later or use a photo instead.
