What to expect from photo recognition and location unlocking.

Updated November 2, 2026

01Exploration and collection

lumiFolio lets you discover, unlock and collect illustrated landmarks, foods and other subjects. Photo and location unlocking are part of the exploration experience.

02Recognition and its limits

Recognition can produce incorrect matches or miss a match; not every photo will unlock an item. Lighting, angle, obstructions, photo quality and reference materials affect the result. Results support in-app exploration and collecting and do not guarantee the identity of the photographed subject. Try another angle, lighting or photo.

03Location results

Location unlocking depends on permission, location accuracy and your surroundings. You may not be able to unlock an item when the location is uncertain or you are not nearby. Try again later or use a photo unlock.

The Apple Standard End User License Agreement applies to the App Store application unless a valid custom license is provided. These feature descriptions do not limit your statutory consumer rights. See the privacy policy for how photos, location and local data are handled.
