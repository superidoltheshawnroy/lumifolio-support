Privacy policy

How photos, location and your collection are handled.

Updated November 19, 2026

This app does not require an account and has no subscriptions, advertising, or third-party analytics.

01Photos and photo library

Photo recognition runs on your device. Photos are not sent to the developer or saved in your collection. They are released when you close the recognition screen; only the record of lighting up a landmark is kept. The system photo picker provides access only to the photo you select. Selecting an iCloud photo may require a system download. Exporting a collectible card creates a Live Photo on your device and adds it to your library with your permission. This permission does not read other photos, and exports are not uploaded to the developer.

02Location and browsing city

Location is optional. It shows your position on the map, finds nearby unlit landmarks, and checks whether you are near a landmark when you tap Light up. You can browse cities, use photo recognition, and view your collection without location permission. Map and landmark check-ins do not save precise coordinates. The app does not track your location in the background. Your selected city is saved on your device. Apple provides maps, system location, and city lookup services, which may use the network. The base map requires a network connection. Apple’s privacy policy applies to those services.

03Custom cards and places

Photos, text, dates and selected places for custom cards are stored on your device. After you choose a photo, its address is used by default when it can be resolved. Otherwise, if location access is already allowed, the app tries to use your current city. You can change or remove the default place. The current-city option does not save your device’s precise coordinates. Address and city lookups use Apple services and require network access. Choosing a city saves the city and its representative location. Choosing a specific place, your current location or the photo’s location saves the selected position with the card. Deleting the card removes its local data.

04Tilt effects

When viewing the details of a card added to your collection, the card can reflect light as you tilt your phone. Motion data is used only for the current view and is neither saved nor uploaded. Motion sensing stops when you leave that view, the app becomes inactive, or you enable Reduce Motion or Reduce Transparency.

05Collection and local data

Your collection, the dates you lit up landmarks, and appearance preferences are stored on your device and are not synced to a developer server. Updating a card’s artwork or text does not remove your progress. Deleting the app in iOS removes its local data; system backup and restore follow your iOS settings.

06Feedback emails

If you email the developer, we receive the address, message and attachments you provide to handle feedback and privacy requests. Emails are not used for advertising. They are retained for handling and necessary follow-up, then deleted when no longer needed unless the law requires retention. The app does not automatically attach photos or precise location.

07Permissions and contact

You can change camera, location and add-to-library permissions in iOS Settings. For feedback, requests to delete feedback emails, or privacy questions, contact the developer at the email address below.
