Privacy policy

How photos, location and your collection are handled.

Updated November 11, 2026

This app does not require an account and has no subscriptions, advertising, or third-party analytics.

01Photos and photo library

Photo recognition runs on your device. Photos are not sent to the developer or saved in your collection. They are released when you close the recognition screen; only the record of lighting up a landmark is kept. The system photo picker provides access only to the photo you select. Selecting an iCloud photo may require a system download.

02Location and browsing city

Location is optional. It shows your position on the map, finds nearby unlit landmarks, and checks whether you are near a landmark when you tap Light up. You can browse cities, use photo recognition, and view your collection without location permission. The app does not save precise coordinates or track you in the background. Your selected city is saved on your device. Apple provides maps, system location, and city lookup services, which may use the network. The base map requires a network connection. Apple’s privacy policy applies to those services.

03Tilt effects

Foil cards reflect light as you tilt your phone. Motion data is used only for the current view and is neither saved nor uploaded. Motion sensing stops when you close the card or enable Reduce Motion.

04Collection and local data

Your collection, the dates you lit up landmarks, and appearance preferences are stored on your device and are not synced to a developer server. Updating a card’s artwork or text does not remove your progress. Deleting the app in iOS removes its local data; system backup and restore follow your iOS settings.

05Feedback emails

If you email the developer, we receive the address, message and attachments you provide to handle feedback and privacy requests. Emails are not used for advertising. They are retained for handling and necessary follow-up, then deleted when no longer needed unless the law requires retention. The app does not automatically attach photos or precise location.

06Permissions and contact

You can change camera and location permissions in iOS Settings. For feedback, requests to delete feedback emails, or privacy questions, contact the developer at the email address below.
