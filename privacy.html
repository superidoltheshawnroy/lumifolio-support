Privacy policy

How photos, location and your collection are handled.

Updated November 2, 2026

This app does not require an account and has no subscriptions, advertising, or third-party analytics.

01Photos and Photo Library

Photos are identified on your device. They are not sent to the developer or saved in your collection. Closing identification releases the photo; only the unlock result is retained. The system photo picker provides only the photo you select. Selecting an iCloud photo may require a system download.

02Location and Exploration Area

Location is optional. It recommends collectibles for your area and checks nearby landmarks when you choose to unlock them. You can explore, identify photos, and view your collection without location permission. The app does not save precise coordinates or track location in the background. It remembers your exploration area on this device. Apple provides system location and city lookup services, which may use the network.

03Device Tilt Effects

Archive details can reflect light as you tilt your device. Motion data is used only for the current display and is neither saved nor uploaded. Sensing stops when you leave details or enable Reduce Motion.

04Collection and Local Data

Your collection, unlock dates, daily collectibles, and appearance preferences are stored on this device and are not synced to a developer server. Updating collectible text or images does not revoke unlock records. Use Delete App in iOS to remove local app data. System backup and restoration follow your iOS settings.

05Feedback emails

If you email the developer, we receive the address, message and attachments you provide to handle feedback and privacy requests. Emails are not used for advertising. They are retained for handling and necessary follow-up, then deleted when no longer needed unless the law requires retention. The app does not automatically attach photos or precise location.

06Permissions and Contact

You can change camera and location permissions in iOS Settings. For feedback, requests to delete feedback emails, or privacy questions, contact the developer at the email address below.
