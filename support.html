01Photos & lighting up

How do I light up a landmark from a photo?

Open an unlit silhouette, tap Light up, and use the Photo tab. Take a photo with the subject aligned to the outline, or choose a photo from your library and confirm the square crop. Recognition runs on your device. Selecting an iCloud photo may require a system download over the internet.

Why didn’t my photo light up a landmark?

To protect your photo privacy, recognition runs on your device without uploading photos or using cloud recognition services. On-device recognition can miss or incorrectly match a landmark. Lighting, angle and obstructions can also affect the result. Try brighter light, another angle, less obstruction or a different photo, with the subject aligned to the outline.

Are my photos uploaded?

Recognition runs on your device. The app does not upload recognition photos to the developer. Apple may download an iCloud photo you select or provide location and city services.

Why didn’t the location check work?

Check location permission and try again near the landmark. An imprecise or uncertain location may prevent the landmark from lighting up. You can use a photo instead.

02Your collection

How do Collection and Timeline work?

In Collection, use the display button to switch between the card carousel and Timeline. Choose all cities or a selection of cities. Tap a card to read its note and drag it or tilt your phone to see the foil reflection.

Where is my collection stored?

Your collection and the dates you lit up landmarks are saved on the device. There is no app account or developer-operated cloud sync. System backup and restoration depend on your iOS settings.

03City exploration

How do I browse and change city colours?

Swipe up or down to choose a city, then tap its card to open the atlas. Pinch the atlas to switch between two, three, and four columns. The colour button in the city atlas changes that city’s colours; Restore city defaults resets only that city.

Can I explore without location access?

Yes. Browse any supported city and light up landmarks using the camera or a photo from your library. Location access is optional.

Does the map work offline?

The map requires a network connection. Without a connection, you can still browse bundled city cards and use on-device photo recognition. You can open the guide again from Settings.
